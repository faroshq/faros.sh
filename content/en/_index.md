{{% blocks/lead color="primary" %}}
Faros transforms Kubernetes cluster management with **AI-powered insights**. Simply deploy our lightweight, read-only agent to your existing clusters, connect your preferred AI foundation model, and instantly discover optimization opportunities, security vulnerabilities, and operational improvements.

**No cluster changes required** – just intelligent analysis and automated remediation at your fingertips.
{{% /blocks/lead %}}

{{% blocks/section color="white" type="row" %}}
{{% blocks/feature icon="fas fa-plug" title="Read-Only Agent" %}}
Deploy our lightweight, non-intrusive agent that safely reads cluster state without any modifications. Zero downtime, zero risk to your production workloads.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}

{{% blocks/feature icon="fas fa-brain" title="AI Foundation Models" %}}
Connect to leading AI providers (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Azure AI) or use your self-hosted models. Choose the AI that fits your security and compliance requirements.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}

{{% blocks/feature icon="fas fa-magic" title="Intelligent Analysis" %}}
AI analyzes your cluster configurations, resource usage, security posture, and best practices violations to provide actionable insights and recommendations.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}
{{% /blocks/section %}}

{{% blocks/section color="dark" type="row" %}}
{{% blocks/feature icon="fas fa-search" title="Deep Cluster Insights" %}}
Discover resource waste, security vulnerabilities, compliance violations, and performance bottlenecks that traditional monitoring tools miss. AI sees patterns humans can't.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}

{{% blocks/feature icon="fas fa-mouse-pointer" title="One-Click Remediation" %}}
Don't just identify problems – fix them instantly. Generate Kubernetes manifests, Helm charts, or automation scripts with a single click. Review before applying.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}

{{% blocks/feature icon="fas fa-shield-alt" title="Security & Compliance" %}}
AI-powered security scans detect misconfigurations, privilege escalations, and policy violations. Automated compliance reporting for SOC2, PCI-DSS, and more.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}
{{% /blocks/section %}}
