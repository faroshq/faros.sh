## Platform

web

## Users

The primary marketing audience is leaders enabling business users and AI agents.
The product also serves developers and platform teams.

## Product Purpose

Faros provides a shared platform for building, running, and governing capabilities
used by people and agents. It combines isolated workspaces, extensible providers,
workspace-scoped MCP tools, and outbound edge connectivity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Providers supply APIs, controllers, backends, portal interfaces, and optional MCP
tools. The portal, CLI, APIs, and MCP access workspace resources. MCP calls inherit
the caller's workspace scope and permissions. These mechanisms are documented in
`content/en/platform/index.md` and the main Faros repository.

The marketing site uses Hugo and existing partials. Product functionality and
claims must be supported by the product documentation; example scenarios are not
customer evidence.

## Brand Commitments

`../faros/docs/design/README.md`, the linked foundation and component contracts,
`../faros/portal/src/assets/main.css`, and canonical PortalKit sources.
